Fast & Simple Installation

Up and running on your MetaTrader terminal in under 60 seconds.

1
Open MetaTrader Data Folder:
In MetaTrader 5, click File > Open Data Folder.
MQL5\Experts\
2
Copy EA File:
Copy ChartThemeStudio_MT5.mq5 (or .ex5) into the folder.
3
Refresh & Attach:
In the Navigator window (Ctrl+N), right-click Expert Advisors and click Refresh, then drag it onto your chart!
1
Open MetaTrader 4 Data Folder:
In MetaTrader 4, click File > Open Data Folder.
MQL4\Experts\
2
Copy & Compile:
Copy ChartThemeStudio_MT4.mq4 into the folder, open MetaEditor (F4) and press Compile (F7).
3
Attach to Chart:
Refresh the Navigator window and drag ChartThemeStudio_MT4 onto any chart.
